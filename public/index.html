How to play

  • Goal: gather every word into its category.
  • Category cards (the coloured ones) go into the empty slots at the top left. Each one shows how many words it needs.
  • Once a category is in a slot, drop its words onto it. When it's full, it clears and frees the slot.
  • In the columns, stack words that share a category, and move those stacks together. Anything can go into an empty column.
  • Two rows layout: there's no deck. A category card can go on top of a stack of its words, and then the whole stack moves together onto matching cards or into a slot.
  • Tap the deck to draw a card. When it runs out, tap again to recycle it. Unlimited.
  • Some words fit more than one category (Orange: fruit or colour?). Any reading is accepted as long as the deal can still be finished.
  • Drag cards, or tap one to pick it up and tap where it should go. Tap a picked-up card again to send it somewhere sensible automatically.
  • Moves: with the move limit on, running out offers a quick mini-game that earns more. It's free, and you can switch the limit off in New deal.
  • Undo is unlimited and free. Keys: Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, Space draw.
